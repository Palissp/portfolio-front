About

I'm a full-stack engineer working independently. Three clients are active right now — HR software, genomics research and education technology — and before those, banking.

Almost everything I've built has gone into systems that already had users — the kind you can't take offline while you improve them. That made me a generalist on purpose. A feature isn't finished until the screen, the API and the database agree, so I'd rather own all three than hand off at the boundary.

It also means I spend real time on the business itself: payroll rules, lab protocols, banking constraints. Code that doesn't understand the domain is code someone rewrites later.

Based in Ecuador, working remotely with teams across the US, Colombia and Uruguay. Open to relocation.
